Pulse brings money and everyday routines into a single mobile-friendly app. It connects bank accounts through Plaid and pairs transaction tracking with budgeting, health logs, and meal planning.

The app is built with Next.js, TypeScript, and Convex, with individual accounts and a home-screen install experience.

Current features include:

- Bank linking and transaction sync through Plaid.
- Personal budgets and spending tracking.
- Health logs and meal planning in the same dashboard.
- Cloud-backed data available across devices.

Pulse is under active development in a private repository.
